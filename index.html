ProjectMar – Oct 2024

Sign Language Robotic Arm top 3

A 3D-printed, tendon-driven arm that watches a person sign and signs it back.

  • A Python script finds the hand with OpenCV, and a classifier turns the static sign into a word.
  • The word maps to a double[16], one angle per servo, from a MySQL sign database.
  • The angles stream over serial to an Arduino, which drives 16 servos that pull tendon lines to form the sign.
PythonOpenCVMLArduino C++MySQL3D printing

ProjectApr – Nov 2023

Helpdesk Robot

A desk robot that wakes up when you say “wake up” and knows who it's talking to.

  • Face recognition checks whether it knows you. If not, it asks your name and saves your photo to a folder under that name.
  • Google speech recognition turns your words into text.
  • The text and your name go to ChatGPT through a bridge, so the spoken reply is tailored to you.
